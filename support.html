Common questions

How do I import my favorites? Export bookmarks from your old browser as an HTML file (Safari, Chrome, Brave, Dia) or JSON (Arc), save it to Files, then in Petal open Menu → Import and choose the file. Petal shows steps for each browser.

How do I split the screen? On an unfolded phone or iPad, turn on Split view in Settings and pick a second tab.

How do I clear history? Settings → Clear history. Deleting the app removes all Petal data.
