Sweb Privacy Policy

HomeSupportPrivacy

Last updated: November 2026

Sweb is a web browser. We built it so your browsing stays yours.

  • No account. You don't need to sign up or log in.
  • On-device by default. Favorites, history, open tabs and settings stay on your device. Your history, tabs and browsing never leave it.
  • Setups you choose to share. When you share your Sweb with Follow on, publish a pack, or set up a Sweb for family, the sites, folder names and look you picked are stored on Sweb's server so others can install them and get your changes. Before anything is published, Sweb flags sites that look personal (banking, mail, health, logins, private links) and leaves them out unless you include them. You can change or delete what you shared at any time.
  • Anonymous device id. To know which setups you own or follow, your phone makes a random id and secret. There is no email, name or phone number, and we don't store IP addresses.
  • Move my Sweb. Move links are encrypted on your phone. The key stays in the link and never reaches the server, which keeps only the unreadable copy for up to 24 hours or until it is used.
  • No tracking or ads. Sweb has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Anonymous counts. A few times a day Sweb sends totals like "2 shares created, 1 pack installed", with no id attached and nothing about which sites or setups. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Imports. When you import bookmarks, Sweb reads the file you pick on your device. The file is never uploaded.
  • Websites you visit. Pages you open can collect data under their own privacy policies, as in any browser. Searches go to the search engine you choose in Settings (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Site icons. To show favorite icons, Sweb asks each favorite's own website for its icon. When a site has none, Sweb asks Google's icon service (t1.gstatic.com) for that domain's icon. Only the domain is sent, never the page or your history.
  • Deleting data. Clear history in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything.
