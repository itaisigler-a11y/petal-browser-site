Petal Privacy Policy

HomeSupportPrivacy

Last updated: November 2026

Petal is a web browser. We built it so your browsing stays yours.

  • No account. You don't need to sign up or log in.
  • On-device only. Favorites, history, open tabs and settings stay on your device. We have no servers that receive them.
  • No tracking or ads. Petal has no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • Imports. When you import bookmarks, Petal reads the file you pick on your device. The file is never uploaded.
  • Websites you visit. Pages you open can collect data under their own privacy policies, as in any browser. Searches go to the search engine you choose in Settings (DuckDuckGo by default).
  • Site icons. To show favorite icons, Petal requests icons.duckduckgo.com/ip3/<domain>.ico for the domains of your favorites.
  • Deleting data. Clear history in Settings. Deleting the app removes everything.
